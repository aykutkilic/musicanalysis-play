The pieces in this folder are the notes (pitch, onset, duration, staff, voice) of the
DCML Mozart Piano Sonatas corpus, converted to this program's format:

  Hentschel, J., Neuwirth, M., Rohrmeier, M. (2021). The Annotated Mozart Sonatas:
  Score, Harmony, and Cadence. Transactions of the International Society for Music
  Information Retrieval 4(1). https://github.com/DCMLab/mozart_piano_sonatas
